# iHungry: Food Website for Broke College Students

A Universal Website that helps people find hidden deals easier. Certain deals are often hard to find because they are not marketed probably. This is often due to because they are only posted on instagram flyers on a small account and spread through word online like reddit posts. Even if they are "marketed properly" some people just don't see them because the deals are fragemented so this app serves as a universal app to find them.
